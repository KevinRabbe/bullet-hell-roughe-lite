# Character Data
- Owns character payloads, starter setup, character-specific hooks, and selectable roster state.
- Characters may react to explicit weapon/mechanical tags, statuses, resources, movement, economy, or other authored systems; they do not own neutral weapon classes or receive automatic class-based shop bias.
- `selectable = false` is the preferred way to park unfinished characters.
- Harvester and Ritualist identity mapping is locked and should not be swapped again.
- Every art-backed hunter must fit the canonical reference combat height defined in `docs/ART_STYLE_RULES.md`.
